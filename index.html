HOW TO PLAY

STAY IN ORBIT

Tap, click or press SPACE to bounce upward. Gravity pulls you back down.

Pass through obstacle segments that match your ball's color. Touch any other color and your run ends.

Collect white stars for points. Grab the four-color orb to switch to a different random color.

Keep climbing. Obstacles get trickier as your score grows.
