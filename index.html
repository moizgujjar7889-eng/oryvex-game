STAY IN ORBIT

Tap, click or press SPACE to bounce up. Gravity pulls you back down.

Pass only through parts that match your ball's color. Touch another color and the run ends.

Collect stars for points. The four-color orb changes your color.

Every 5 obstacles is a new level with new shapes and faster spins.

Spend 5 ✦ to continue once per run. Infinity gives you 3 lives. Hard is faster and tighter. Daily is one shared course for everyone today.
