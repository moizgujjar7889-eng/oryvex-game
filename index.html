HOW TO PLAY

STAY IN ORBIT

Tap, click or press SPACE to bounce up. Gravity pulls you back down.

You can only pass through parts that match your ball's color. Touch any other color and you lose.

Collect stars for points and to save up. The four-color orb changes your color.

Spend 5 ✦ to continue once per run. Infinity gives you 3 lives. Hard is faster and tighter.
